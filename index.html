§ 01

Four ways to capture depth.
Six ways to dress it.

A — Live LiDAR

Point, freeze, weave.

On iPhone Pro, the LiDAR scanner streams a real-time depth field. Tap to freeze a frame and feed it straight into the generator.

B — On-device AI

Depth from any photo.

Depth Anything V2 runs on the Neural Engine. Pick a picture, get a depth map. Nothing uploads — the model is bundled in the app.

C — 3D models

USDZ, USD, OBJ, SCN.

Drop a 3D file in, orbit the camera, capture the view-space depth. Bundled samples ship with the app to play with right away.

D — Patterns

Random dot to reaction-diffusion.

Six procedural generators (random dot, stars, Perlin, Worley, Voronoi, reaction-diffusion) plus your own photos as tiles.

§ 02

Tune every knob.

DPI, depth strength, separation, oversampling. Remap input and output ranges to push subjects forward or back. Denoise quantized 8-bit imports for clean, banding-free output. Invert depth in one tap. The preview redraws as you slide.

Under the hood: a parallelised version of the Thimbleby–Inglis–Witten autostereogram algorithm with W. A. Steer's link-based hidden-surface refinement. Powered by ARKit, Core ML, SceneKit, Metal, and Core Image. Output is upscaled so the minimum dimension is at least 960 px — print-ready or share-ready.

§ 03

Everything runs on device. No account, no upload, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no ads. Your photos and 3D scans never leave your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
